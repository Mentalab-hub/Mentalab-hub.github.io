ExG Visualization

The ExG tab shows the ExG signals of your device in real time. It opens automatically after you connect to a device.

ExG visualization tab

The ExG visualization tab displays ExG data in real time.

Header

The header above the signals shows the time window, for example ↔ 10s, and the y-axis scale, for example ↕ 100 uV.

While data is streamed via LSL, the header also shows LSL. While recording, it shows a pink dot and a timer with the recording time.

Signal display

The ExG tab shows one trace per channel. The traces are labelled CH1, CH2, and so on. If you assigned electrode positions on the IMPEDANCE tab, the electrode names are shown instead.

Up to 8 channels fit on the screen. For 16-channel and 32-channel devices, scroll up and down to see all channels.

New data is drawn from left to right and overwrites the oldest data. A grey vertical line marks the current position.

The displayed data is filtered with the bandpass and notch filters from the Settings page.

Filters only affect the display. Recorded data and data streamed via LSL are always raw.

To change the filters, see Filter settings.

Markers

Event markers from the device are shown as pink vertical lines with a label.

To learn how to set a marker with the device button, see Button Commands and Functions (marker, shutdown, reset).

Markers are also saved in recordings and streamed via LSL. You cannot create markers in the app.

Bottom Hamburger Menu

Tap the dot menu in the bottom right corner to open the menu. Tap the arrow to close it again.

The menu contains the following buttons, from left to right:

Button Function

Padlock

Lock the screen. See Lock the screen.

Wave

Start streaming via LSL. While streaming, the button shows a power icon. Tap it to stop streaming. See Lab Streaming Layer.

Sliders

Open the visualization settings.

Play

Start a recording. While recording, the button shows a stop icon. Tap it to stop the recording. See Recording.
ExG menu and visualization settings

The open menu at the bottom and the visualization settings dialog.

Visualization settings

Tap the sliders button in the menu to open the Visualization Settings dialog. Here you can set the y-axis scale between 1 uV and 100 mV, and the time window to 5 s, 10 s, or 20 s. The defaults are 100 uV and 10 s. Tap Apply to apply the settings.

The visualization settings are kept while the app is open. When you restart the app, they are reset to their defaults.

Lock the screen

Lock the screen to prevent accidental touches, for example while the phone is in your pocket during a recording.

  1. Tap the padlock button in the menu.

  2. Tap Confirm in the Lock screen? dialog.

    The screen dims and ignores all touches. Recording and LSL streaming continue.

  3. To see the screen, tap it once. The screen brightens for a few seconds and shows the hint Triple-tap to unlock.

  4. To unlock the screen, tap it three times quickly.

If the device disconnects, the screen is unlocked automatically.
